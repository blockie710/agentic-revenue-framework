## §3. How This Multiplies a Federal Win
1. **Non-dilutive leverage.** $25K (Phase I) / $125K (Phase II) reimburses qualifying R&D spend on top of the federal award — extends runway without equity.
2. **De-risks the federal budget.** Known state match lets the firm allocate federal dollars to higher-risk tasks, using ETF reimbursement for documented direct costs.
3. **Signals viability.** The ETF Letter of Support (if obtained pre-submission) strengthens the NSF proposal's commercialization narrative.
4. **Pure upside.** No federal award → no ETF obligation; the only cost is application/admin time. There is **no downside** to pursuing it.
